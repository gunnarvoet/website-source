{{/* ------------------------------------------------------------------ A page's featured image, as a full-bleed plate. Academic's `image.preview_only` means the picture is a listing thumbnail and does not belong on the page itself, so honour it and render nothing. The plate fills the band. Several of the cruise headers are narrow crops (Palau is 1196px, T-TIDE 800px) and letting the browser upscale a wide ocean photograph costs nothing visible, whereas capping them at their own pixels leaves a page-wide band with paper showing down both sides. Below the threshold the picture is not a band image at all but an illustration that happens to sit in `featured.*` (the conda post's XKCD comic is 492px of line art, which pixelates the moment it is stretched), so those stay at native size, centered on the paper. ------------------------------------------------------------------ */}} {{- $shot := .Resources.GetMatch "featured.*" -}} {{- $meta := .Params.image | default dict -}} {{- if and $shot (not $meta.preview_only) -}} {{- $img := $shot -}} {{- if gt $shot.Width 2400 }}{{ $img = $shot.Resize "2400x" }}{{ end -}} {{- end -}}
